Summary

State-of-the-art hand mesh reconstruction systems (HaMeR, CVPR 2024) are benchmarked almost exclusively on FreiHAND — a Western-participant dataset. Korean hand morphology differs measurably: HaMeR's PA-MPJPE rises from 6.5mm (FreiHAND) to 8.14mm on Korean inputs — a 45% accuracy loss for a Korean deployment.

The naïve fix — fine-tuning HaMeR on Korean data — closes the gap to 5.78mm but causes +39% regression on FreiHAND and +48% on InterHand2.6M. Neither full fine-tuning nor LoRA avoids this: the trade-off is intrinsic to mano_head adaptation, not a parameter-budget problem.

Our approach: specialize additively, not by overwriting. We train a 22M-parameter specialist alongside frozen HaMeR and blend their per-rotation predictions at inference via a learned SLERP gating MLP. The gating automatically defers to HaMeR on Western inputs (preserving its accuracy) while blending in the specialist on Korean inputs. Result: PA-MPJPE 5.31mm on Korean hands, zero FreiHAND regression, 28× fewer parameters than HaMeR's backbone.

Results

All results on a 3,000-frame, 15-subject, subject-disjoint Korean test partition (no training-set subjects). The learned-α ensemble beats HaMeR-FT (full 40M-param fine-tune) on all 25 grasp types, all 27 object categories, and all 15 test subjects.

Key Findings

1
The specialisation trade-off is intrinsic to mano_head adaptation. Both full fine-tuning (40M) and LoRA (0.9M) regress FreiHAND by +29–39%. The problem is the adaptation objective, not the parameter count.
2
Additive specialisation avoids the trade-off entirely. Keeping HaMeR frozen and training a separate small specialist preserves FreiHAND accuracy exactly while delivering best-in-class Korean performance.
3
Population-specific MANO templates matter beyond fine-tuning. The Korean template (fit to 4,532 scans) contributes ~1.5mm PA-MPVPE improvement independently of the ensemble — it is not a cosmetic preference.
4
Gating learns biomechanically meaningful α values. Wrist (joint 0): α ≈ 1.0 (defer fully to HaMeR's global rotation). Finger joints: α ≈ 0.55 (genuine blend). The system self-discovers this without supervision.

Technical Stack

Architecture
  • DINOv2-S (last 6 blocks fine-tuned)
  • SemGCN (21 landmarks)
  • Cross-attention fusion
  • IEF rotation head (5 steps)
  • SLERP on SO(3)¹⁶
  • Gating MLP (700K params)
Data & Training
  • AIHub Korean Hand (92K frames)
  • FreiHAND · HO-3D · DexYCB
  • Korean MANO template (4,532 scans)
  • AdamW · SGD · ~6h on RTX 4090
  • Geodesic + Frobenius rotation loss
  • HaMeR distillation
