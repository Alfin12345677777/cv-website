Research Interests

3D Vision & Mesh Reconstruction

Hand and body mesh reconstruction from RGB. MANO & SMPL-X parametric models. Forward kinematics, linear blend skinning, and the gap between direct vertex regression and rotation-driven generation.

Medical Computer Vision

3D structure recovery for diagnostic and rehabilitation aids. Anatomically valid outputs as a first-class constraint rather than a post-hoc check.

Generative & Spatial Modeling

Diffusion and flow-based 3D generation (actively learning — score matching, DDPM, flow matching). World modeling and spatial intelligence as frameworks for grounding 3D understanding. Cross-modal fusion of visual tokens and structural priors via cross-attention. Not yet an expert in generative models, but this is the direction I'm building toward from my current 3D reconstruction work.

Korean NLP

Multi-label emotion classification with KoELECTRA / BERT. Semi-supervised pseudo-labeling for class imbalance. Threshold optimization and ensemble methods.

Publications

  1. ACCV 2026

    KoreanHand: Population-Specialized Hand Mesh Reconstruction

    Alfin W. A. Putra, X. P. Yang
    Asian Conference on Computer Vision (ACCV) 2026. Under review.

    A 22 M-parameter specialist (DINOv2-S + SemGCN + cross-attention + IEF) trained on 92 K Korean hand frames is ensembled with frozen HaMeR via a learned per-rotation SLERP gating MLP. The ensemble achieves PA-MPJPE 5.31mm / PA-MPVPE 5.14mm on a 3,000-frame subject-disjoint Korean test set — beating full fine-tuning of HaMeR (40 M params, 5.78 mm) by 8.1% — while maintaining exact zero regression on FreiHAND (6.50 mm). A population-specific Korean MANO template fit to 4,532 scans contributes an independent −1.5 mm PA-MPVPE gain.

KoreanHand — Population-Specialized Hand Mesh Reconstruction

Instead of fine-tuning a foundation model (which causes +39% regression on Western benchmarks), I trained a 22 M-parameter specialist and ensembled it with frozen HaMeR via a learned per-rotation SLERP gating MLP. Result: PA-MPJPE 5.31mm on Korean hands, beating full fine-tuning by 8.1%, with zero FreiHAND regression. A Korean MANO template fit to 4,532 scans contributes an independent −1.5mm gain. 28× smaller than the foundation model.

2026 — present · research · MICCAI 2027

Anatomy-Guided Adaptive Partitioning for Multi-Organ CT Segmentation

Extends MagicNet (CVPR 2023) by replacing its uniform grid with an anatomy-aware adaptive octree — a frozen TotalSegmentator prior drives finer cube subdivision where small organs and boundaries live. In the semi-supervised regime (10% labels, 4 scans), the octree reliably improves tail-organ DSC: pancreas +2.5, esophagus +3.0 (stable across 3 seeds). Multi-seed evaluation overturned two of our own hypotheses, including a "regression" that turned out to be pure seed noise (right adrenal ±0.068).
